# Futu Visual Ground Truth — As-Of Amendment v1

Status: **MANDATORY INTERPRETATION CORRECTION**
Date: 2026-10-02

The five supplied screenshots are authoritative evidence of how Futu rendered
SSSS and ADKBY-E **as of the screenshots' latest available bar**.

They are not direct evidence of what the historical rails/colors/icons looked
like when each old bar first closed.

Reason:
XMA is centered/repainting. When later bars arrive, historical XMA values near
the former right edge can revise.

Therefore:

- screenshot comparison must use `RENDER_ASOF`;
- causal historical research must use `FIRST_OBSERVED`;
- a historical icon visible in a current screenshot must not automatically be
  treated as a signal that was observable on that historical date.

This distinction supersedes any earlier wording that could be read as requiring
current screenshot history to equal the first-observed ledger bar-for-bar.
