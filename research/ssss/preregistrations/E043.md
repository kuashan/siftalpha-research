## 7. Initial causal decision policy for forward paper trading

This is intentionally conservative and may later be revised only under a new logged version.

OPEN LONG requires:
- at least one bullish STRUCTURE event from F1 or causal F2;
- old SSSS directional context not structurally bearish;
- at least one MOMENTUM confirmation from F3 or F4;
- no active severe distribution / exhaustion conflict.

OPEN SHORT:
symmetric bearish definition.

ADD requires:
- position already open;
- thesis direction still structurally valid;
- a fresh local pullback / recovery event;
- no severe opposite distribution signal;
- margin cap respected.

REDUCE requires:
- position profitable OR already materially exposed;
- at least two independent risk families deteriorate;
- thesis is not yet fully invalidated.

CLOSE requires one of:
- structural invalidation;
- hard risk stop;
- confirmed opposite structural signal;
- liquidation-buffer protection.

These are execution scaffolds, not accepted alpha rules.

## 8. Hard risk controls

Because the instrument setting is 10x:

Per-position hard stop:
the tighter of:
- 2.0 x 5m ATR from weighted entry;
- a move that would consume 1.25% of total account equity on current notional.

No ADD may move the hard stop farther away in account-risk terms.

Daily paper loss stop:
-3% account equity.

Portfolio peak-to-trough kill switch:
-6% during E043 forward paper trading.

After kill switch:
no new paper OPEN until the next UTC day.

Liquidation-buffer rule:
estimated liquidation distance must remain at least 2x the current hard-stop distance.

If this cannot be satisfied:
position size must be reduced before any new ADD.
