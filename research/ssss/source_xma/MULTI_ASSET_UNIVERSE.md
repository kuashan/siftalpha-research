# Multi-Asset Research Universe

Status: DISCOVERY UNIVERSE  
Created: 2026-09-28

## Purpose

The strategy must not be judged from one symbol.

The next exploratory rounds will run the same point-in-time Source-XMA process across a group of widely recognized, liquid, economically meaningful US-listed securities.

This is a **Discovery universe**, not Frozen OOS.

Selection itself is not treated as unbiased validation.

## Why the universe is mixed

A strategy that works only on one type of high-beta technology stock is not considered general.

The first wave deliberately contains:
- growth tech,
- mature tech,
- semiconductors,
- healthcare,
- financials,
- energy.

## Decision rules

1. Long, short, flat, reduce, and add are all allowed outcomes.
2. **No requirement exists to open a short position.**
3. A sell/exit signal is not automatically a short-entry signal.
4. A high-side extreme is not automatically bearish.
5. Position size is determined from the available point-in-time evidence.
6. Auxiliary factors may increase or decrease confidence, and may also be rejected as harmful.

## Capital convention

For single-symbol case studies:
- nominal initial capital = $10,000 per symbol

For future portfolio-level studies:
- do not silently give every symbol a separate $10,000 and call the sum one portfolio;
- use one explicit portfolio capital pool and record cross-symbol capital competition.

## Research sequencing

### Phase A — per-symbol replay
Use $10,000 nominal capital independently to understand each symbol's signal mechanics.

### Phase B — cross-symbol comparison
Compare which signal families generalize and which are symbol-specific.

### Phase C — portfolio simulation
Only after cross-symbol rules are frozen:
- one shared capital pool
- explicit max gross exposure
- explicit per-name cap
- explicit correlation / concentration constraints

This avoids optimizing portfolio sizing before the signal logic is stable.
